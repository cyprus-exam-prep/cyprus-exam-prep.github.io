Topic 5: Political system, powers, parties, office-holders (Πολίτευμα, Εξουσίες, Κόμματα)

What you need to know about political system, powers, parties, office-holders for the Cyprus citizenship and long-term residence exam on the Contemporary Political and Social Reality of Cyprus. Key Greek words in bold.

  • Προεδρική δημοκρατία (presidential republic). The President is head of state and head of government, elected for five years. There is no Prime Minister.
  • The President appoints the Υπουργικό Συμβούλιο (Council of Ministers). There are 11 ministries plus deputy ministries.
  • Βουλή των Αντιπροσώπων: one chamber, 80 seats (56 Greek Cypriot, 24 reserved for Turkish Cypriots and vacant since 1964), five-year term.
  • Independent officers: Γενικός Εισαγγελέας (Attorney-General), Γενικός Ελεγκτής (Auditor-General), Επίτροπος Διοικήσεως (Ombudsman). Highest court: the Supreme Court.
