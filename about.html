About CyprusExamPrep

A Cyprus-based independent project that helps people prepare for the exam on the basic elements of the contemporary political and social reality of Cyprus, needed for naturalisation and long-term residence. We are not part of, or endorsed by, the Ministry of Education or any government body.

How we write the questions

  • From the official syllabus. Every question maps to one of the 14 topic areas in the Ministry of Education's official syllabus.
  • Every answer checked against an official source. Each answer links to the page it comes from: gov.cy, the Ministry of Education, the Deputy Ministry of Tourism's visitcyprus.com, or a named published source. If we can't source it, we don't publish it.
  • Who's who is dated and re-checked before each sitting. Ministers, mayors and office-holders change. Our Who's who carries the date it was verified and is checked again before every exam date.
  • Written in exam Greek, with English and Russian translations, because the exam itself is in Greek only.
  • No invented "real exam" questions. The Ministry publishes no past papers, and we never claim a question was on the exam.
