Which one fits you

You want to spend nothing

  • Start with the official syllabus (free, one page) and the visitcyprus.com practical-info pages it recommends.
  • Then test yourself for free: our 15-question test (Greek with English/Russian) and the free questions on open-exam-prep.com (English only; check its exam facts against the official ones).
  • Our 14 topic summaries, Who's who and Greek trap words are free too.

You read Greek slowly

  • Practise with questions printed in Greek, not only in translation. Our free test and pack put Greek first; the Amazon book is bilingual English–Greek.
  • If reading is the real obstacle, a few lessons with a Greek tutor may help more than another question bank.

You prefer an app on your phone

  • “About Cyprus” is free to download, offers Greek, English and Russian, and has a paid premium tier.
  • Skip “Ιθαγένεια Quiz” for this exam: it covers Greece's citizenship exam, a different syllabus.

You want a structured, timed rehearsal

  • The Amazon book (bilingual, 10 mocks stated) or our €12 pack (99 Greek questions with EN/RU, 2 timed mocks, Who's who refreshed before every sitting, 30-day refund).
