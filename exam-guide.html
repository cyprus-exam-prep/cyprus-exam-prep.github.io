What the exam covers

Questions come from 14 syllabus areas: current affairs, modern history since 1960, customs and traditions, geography, the political system and office-holders, the economy, entry requirements, telecommunications, climate, languages and religions, currency, holidays and working hours, health and safety, and transport.

How to prepare

  1. Read the 14 topic summaries and learn the Greek key words.
  2. Learn exam Greek: many mistakes come from misreading words like ανακριβές (inaccurate) or ΔΕΝ (not).
  3. Take the free 15-question test to see where you stand.
  4. Practise full 25-question mocks against a 45-minute timer.
  5. In the last week, re-check current office-holders: ministers and party leaders change.
