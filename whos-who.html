Changed in 2026

  • 6 Aug 2026 — cabinet reshuffle. Transport: Ευανθία Τσολάκη (replaced Αλέξης Βαφεάδης). Agriculture: Χρίστος Σενέκης (replaced Μαρία Παναγιώτου). Social Welfare: Τίνα Παύλου. Culture: Κλέα Χατζηστεφάνου Παπαέλληνα (moved from Social Welfare; replaced Βασιλική Κασσιανίδου). Announced 4 Aug, sworn in 6 Aug. Politis · Cyprus Mail
  • 31 Jul 2026 — Deputy Ministry for European Affairs closed. Its term ended after Cyprus's EU Council presidency; Μαριλένα Ραουνά left the government. Cyprus Mail
  • 4 Jun 2026 — House President re-elected. Αννίτα Δημητρίου won a second term with 29 votes, after the parliamentary election of 24 May 2026. parliament.cy · ΡΙΚ
  • 5 Feb 2026 — Paphos mayor suspended. Φαίδωνας Φαίδωνος was placed on suspension (αργία) under the Municipalities Law; Αντιδήμαρχος Άγγελος Ονησιφόρου acts as mayor (Δημαρχεύων). Kathimerini (ΚΥΠΕ) · pafos.org.cy

How to use this page

  • Learn the Greek title as well as the name: the exam is in Greek and asks things like «Ποιος είναι ο Γενικός Ελεγκτής της Δημοκρατίας;».
  • Watch look-alike titles: Υπουργός (minister) vs Υφυπουργός (deputy minister); Γενικός Εισαγγελέας (Attorney-General) vs Γενικός Ελεγκτής (Auditor-General). More in Greek trap words.
  • Re-check one week before your sitting. Office-holders change; this page is dated for that reason.
