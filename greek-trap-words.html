Greek trap words for the Cyprus citizenship exam

The exam on the Contemporary Political and Social Reality of Cyprus is written in Greek: 25 multiple-choice questions, 45 minutes, pass mark 15/25. Many wrong answers come from the Greek wording, not the facts: two terms a few letters apart, or a word well above B1 level. These 44 terms cover the traps reported from real papers, with English and Russian.
